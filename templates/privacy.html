Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and notes.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, needed to run the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate Spendly, show you your expenses and spending summaries, keep your account secure, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal data or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is retained for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including storing passwords in hashed form. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your account or expense data with other third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
